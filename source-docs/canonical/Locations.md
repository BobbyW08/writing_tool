# Locations — The Traveler

*World: Maren | Pre-Pangea continental breakup. Three major continents plus smaller landmasses. Not rowboat distance but not months by ship — a few days to a few weeks crossing.*

## THE WORLD: MAREN

The world Sero inhabits. Same physics and nature as real life. No supernatural forces. Human-only population. Set in a pre-Pangea continental breakup timeframe — continents are aware of each other's existence and have established trade and conflict across the seas.

**Technology arc:** Medieval at the start. Evolves through 2,000 years toward early industrial by the end of Sero's story.

## CALDOR

**Status:** ✓ Locked

*The main continent. Where most stories take place.*

Caldor is the central stage. It was already a patchwork of kingdoms and city-states when the Asharan Empire arrived. Over 2,000 years it goes from conquered territory to fragmented ruin to rebuilt civilization. At its peak, the empire controlled roughly 50–70% of Caldor — Caeldrath remained the sole holdout.

**Continental geography (rough):** Mountains in the north and interior. Fertile plains in the center. River networks running to the coasts. The western coast faces the calmer sea toward Keth. The eastern and southern coasts face rougher open water.

### **Renmark**

**Status:** ✓ Locked

Sero's homeland. A coastal port city on the western edge of Caldor, facing the calmer sea toward Keth. Mediterranean feel — warm, busy, salt-aired. One of the first cities the Asharan Empire landed at when they crossed the sea.

**Significance to Sero:**

  - Where he was born and grew up
  - Where he met Giana and Vashten
  - Where the ritual was performed
  - Where the empire's primary archive is located (the one that extended Nyneave's life)
  - The emotional root of everything that follows

**The Renmark Archive:** The empire's primary records archive, established early in the conquest. Has an unusual property — people assigned to work within its proximity age more slowly. This is not a Star-Farer property. It is specific to this one archive. The mechanism is not fully understood even by Sero. \[\[Characters\#Nyneave|Nyneave\]\] lived within its radius for ~230 years. When she left, she died within months.

### **The Imperial Capital**

**Status:** ~ Needs a name

The administrative center of Asharan rule on Caldor. Not Renmark — a separate city, likely more centrally located on the continent for administrative control. Seat of the Empress. Location of the great library (built ~Age 440). Location of Vashten's office and residence.

**At the time of the collapse (~Age 820):** The city is in suspension — not fully destroyed, not functional. Multiple factions negotiating. Markets still running. Courts still meeting. The empress has been dead two days when Sero and Bard arrive.

**The invasion:** The \[\[Locations\#The Mountain People's Homeland|Mountain People\]\] invade from the northwest. The city burns. Not systematically — as a byproduct of fighting and looting. The port infrastructure (docks, warehouses, harbor) is preserved because that's what they came for.

**After the fall:** The invaders establish a coastal garrison and a new settlement slightly along the coast. The ruins of the old city are largely abandoned. Over time, people drift back to the ruins. A shadow city grows in the rubble.

### **The Underground City**

**Status:** ~ In development

*No name yet — the people living here need to name it themselves.*

The tunnel network beneath the imperial capital, predating the city above. Originally built for drainage, defense, storage. Fell out of use as the empire built better systems above ground. Sero knows they exist because he was here when they were constructed — or before they were abandoned.

**The evacuation (~Age 820):** Sero leads thousands of civilians through these tunnels as the city burns above them. Northwest exit into the hills — the worst possible direction, toward the Mountain People's territory, but the only way out.

**What it becomes:** People who emerged on the coast headed south. But some went back. And some never left the tunnels. The underground community forms in the ruins — beneath the notice of the invaders above. Growing in the dark. Like the Fremen sietches — shaped entirely by the constraints of their environment. No sunlight. Limited resources. The need for secrecy. These constraints become strengths over time.

**What's down there beyond the tunnels:** The engineer who starts mapping finds the tunnels go deeper and older than Sero knew. What's at the bottom is an open question.

**Significance over time:** Sero returns here across centuries. It becomes the closest thing he has to a permanent home. The underground community becomes one of the most important civilizations in the post-collapse world — invisible to surface powers, preserving knowledge, developing their own culture.

### **Caeldrath**

**Status:** ✓ Locked

A massive walled coastal kingdom on the far side of Caldor from Renmark. Ruled by one family for the entirety of Sero's known timeline. Never conquered by the Asharan Empire. The empire made one diplomatic approach — Caeldrath refused. The empire routed around them.

**The walls:** Hundreds of miles of walls closing off a massive area. Not a city — a kingdom with walls at its borders. The geography helps: their terrain is defensible. But the walls are also deliberate political statement.

**What they chose:** 2,000 years of uninterrupted peace by refusing to engage with empires, wars, or external powers. The cost: isolation. The benefit: they have advanced further than any other civilization on Maren through that uninterrupted peace.

**On maps:** Represented by a dashed wall line on the far edge of Caldor rather than a coastline — indicating their closed borders. The ocean is behind the walls; they face inward.

**Sero's relationship with Caeldrath:**

  - Attempted entry twice (~Ages 680–700). Denied both times.
  - The specific person who denies him needs to be a real character — not just an obstacle.
  - Eventually gains entry. What changed? What did he offer or demonstrate?
  - What he finds inside resets his understanding of what peace can actually produce.

**After the collapse:** Caeldrath formally denounces the empire (~Age 775) and closes even its single trade port. Begins accepting Caldori refugees (~Age 920) — becomes an unintentional repository of Caldori culture.

### **The Interior — Star-Farer Archive Location**

**Status:** ✓ Locked | Exact location secret

Remote interior of Caldor. A sealed underground chamber. Found by Sero at approximately Age 55 while on an imperial assignment in the region. The chamber belongs to the Star-Farers — an advanced pre-human (or early-human) civilization that left the planet entirely.

**What's inside:** Technology that makes no sense for the era. Documents in a language Sero spends decades learning to partially translate. The photograph — a flat rigid surface bearing a perfect image of people standing before a structure of metal and fire reaching into the sky.

**The photograph:** Stays locked in the archive. Sero does not carry it. He is the only living person who knows it exists.

**Later visits:** On a second visit (roughly Age 400–500), Sero finds something he missed the first time. On a third visit, something else. Each return to the archive changes what he thinks the Star-Farers actually were.

**What the archive contains that matters most:** References to three other archive locations on Caldor, Keth, and Mossend. Advanced science in medicine, chemistry, and materials that Sero uses selectively — never revealing the source.

## KETH

**Status:** ✓ Locked

*The empire's homeland. Hotter, drier, closer to the equator.*

The Asharan Empire originated here. The home continent of the civilization that crossed the sea and conquered Caldor. More advanced than Caldor in almost every respect when the conquest happened.

**Climate:** Hot, arid. Different ecosystem from Caldor. The settlers who came to Caldor brought their diseases with them unknowingly — not through malice but through immunity they didn't know they had.

**The Imperial Capital on Keth:** The seat of true Asharan power. Older than the Caldor administrative capital. The deep archives are here — records of the empire going back further than anyone on Caldor knew.

### **The Keth Archive**

**Status:** ✓ Locked

Found by Sero on his first journey to Keth (~Age 380). The empire's deep historical records. Where he discovered:

  - The empire is far older than anyone knew
  - The ritual's full three-element structure
  - The words of the ritual ceremony
  - References to the Star-Farers existing in imperial consciousness

**What he couldn't find here:** The third element of the ritual. What it is. Who holds it at any given time.

**Later visits:** Third visit (~Ages 1420–1500) during the empire's slow decline. The empire on Keth survives Sero's timeline but contracts continuously.

## MOSSEND

**Status:** ✓ Locked

*The third continent. Virtually uninhabited.*

Vast forests. Tropical regions. Extraordinary wildlife. Civilization doesn't reach it until hundreds of years after Sero first visits.

**Sero's first visit (~Age 900):** Crosses to the uninhabited continent alone. Finds the third archive. Spends months there. Does not write about this journey in his ledger — it is too personal, too overwhelming. The only journey he refuses to document.

**The Mossend Archive:** Third archive in the Star-Farer network. What makes it different from the others is not yet defined.

**Later:** First permanent settlements appear ~Age 1300. Volcanic event ~Age 1800 slows colonization for a generation.

## THE MOUNTAIN PEOPLE'S HOMELAND

**Status:** ~ In development | Needs a name

A mountainous region west-northwest of the imperial capital city on Caldor. An inland, landlocked civilization. They have always wanted the coastal port — not from grievance or historical claim but from pure strategic necessity. The port represents their gateway to ocean trade, naval capacity, and the economic expansion they've been denied by geography.

**Why they waited:** They could not challenge the Asharan Empire at its height. They've been watching, building military capacity, waiting for the window.

**The window (~Age 820):** The empress is dead. The soldiers have abandoned their posts. The supply chains have collapsed. They move.

**Their method:** Not occupation — strategic acquisition. They want the port and its infrastructure. They burn everything that represents the empire's cultural identity. The docks, warehouses, and harbor they preserve.

**After the invasion:** They establish a coastal garrison and a new settlement along the coast — building fresh, away from the ruins, establishing their own identity in the new territory. The ruins of the old city are largely ignored.

**Cultural character:** Mountain people. Resource-conscious. Practical. Possibly religious about the mountains — the high places as sacred. They've been landlocked forever. Their entire cultural framework doesn't include the ocean. What happens to them psychologically when they finally have it?
