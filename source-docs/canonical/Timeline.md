# PERIOD 1

## PRE-CONQUEST — RENMARK

  - Sero starts work at the scribe shop. Menas, the shop, the daily grind established.
  - Sero meets Giana. She's better than him and doesn't pretend otherwise.
  - Sero makes his feelings about scribe work known, loudly and often.
  - Giana reframes it. The work isn't about ink and contracts — it's about information. She knows more about this city than anyone. Who owes who. Who's lying. Who's struggling. Sero finds this interesting despite himself.
  - Sero has somewhere he goes alone after work. The docks, a wall overlooking the sea, somewhere. His thinking place.
  - Days pass. Rhythm of the shop. Clients come in — merchants, minor officials, ordinary people. Sero starts paying attention the way Giana taught him. He hates that it's working.
  - An old friend arrives — a sailor, someone Sero grew up with. His ship leaves tomorrow. He wants Sero on it. There's something out there, he says. Come see it.
  - Sero almost goes. He doesn't. He tells himself it's nothing. It's Giana.
  - The next morning Renmark wakes up to Asharan ships in the harbor. The invasion begins.
  - Sero learns his friend's ship was sunk trying to flee. No survivors.
  - Sero's grief hardens into hatred for the Asharan. They took the only exit he didn't take.
  - The local lords fail catastrophically — too proud, too territorial to unite. Renmark falls with barely a fight. Twelve leaders are publicly executed. Fealty is demanded of everyone else.

## THE CONQUEST — ASHARAN OCCUPATION

  - Sero is captured working late at the shop. Treated with indifference, not cruelty. That almost makes it worse.
  - The Asharan begin sorting through Renmark's population for usefulness. Sero's literacy puts him in a different category.
  - Sero is brought in to help the empire make sense of local records — contracts, land ownership, debts, governance. He's good at it and resents being good at it.
  - Sero meets Vashten. The only Asharan who speaks to him like a person rather than a resource. Sero doesn't trust him immediately.
  - Vashten and Sero's relationship builds slowly. Sero realizes Vashten is different — older somehow, steadier, without ambition. He's curious about this man.
  - Giana and Sero grow closer during the occupation. The world has gotten smaller. He stops pretending he doesn't care about her.
  - The Immortality Ritual. Sero is told it is ceremonial — an oath of service to the Empire. He doesn't know what it actually is. Nobody tells him.
  - Sero is appointed to an official position. Record keeper, historian, information collector. He travels for the empire. Vashten travels with him or nearby.
