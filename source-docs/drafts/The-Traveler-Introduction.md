# Tab 1

Month/Year

I awoke this morning to the sound of the tavern mistress chasing young ladies out of my bed. Indeed, what a life I lead. As I began to look for my clothes, my head felt very heavy, and I was having an awful time trying to keep it upright. My head and shoulders seemed to sway off the bed, while my body seemed to sway the other direction. I quickly found myself on the floor, face-first, but I had found my pants. After giving up on movement, I collected my thoughts. Then it struck me. Last evening, after another rousing performance…. Oh shit….

A quick rummage through my pants pockets, and oh thank the sweet heavens, the coin purse is still here. There certainly seems to be some well-to-do people in this part of the world. Good to make note of that. Now if I could only remember the name.

As the events of the evening were slowly finding their way to the forefront of my memory, one of the first things to resurface was that of my chance encounter. As I can recall, directly after my performance, I was passing the hat around and accepting my well wishes from the crowd. I asked the keeper for an ale, when I was quickly informed there was a specific man looking to meet with me. I sharply informed the barkeep that I was not interested in those types of meetings. The bartender pulled in close, "He didn't seem the asking type; there is always the back door?"

He glanced over my shoulder and nodded towards where a strange-looking fellow was sitting quietly, all alone in a corner. He was obviously an Asharan, but that is not why he appeared strange. He looked distinctively weathered and without a hint of doubt in himself or a single worry on his mind. However, his clothes had noticeable wear and looked as if he had inherited them from a much older man.

He generously praised my performance and flair for the dramatic. He also tipped generously and offered to buy me a round. Then, as we discussed recent travels, where I had been coming from, what news I had of the world, his demeanor seemed to suddenly change. He was certainly not listening to the news I was so politely and succinctly providing.

He began scanning the room with quiet, deliberate eyes. Not nervously, but as if he was checking who was near and if we could be heard or not. He obviously had a topic on his mind, and he wasn't interested in divulging it to any watching patrons. He asked me to join him in the private room just off the main hall.

"Let's continue this conversation in the sitting; I have it reserved for the entire evening."

Both he and I went to stand. I informed him I was flattered but preferred to take my leave. I thanked him for the generous donation and the drink and went to make my exit.

At this point, he grabbed my arm and said, "I am sorry to do this. But please come with me. I'm an official advisor to the empress. I would like to continue our conversation." Without a sound, a few of their stone-faced warriors had appeared throughout the tavern. Now I was the one surveying the exits. He showed me to a hallway just behind the table he sat at.

He was not sorry and yet continued cordially. Then I understood his silent confidence. This was a man of power and control. I was not going to be able to leave this conversation without his approval.

He led me to a small private parlor just off the main hall. A fire had already been lit. Two chairs faced it. He settled into one as though he had sat in it a thousand times before and gestured to the other.

"What is your name?" He asked almost suspiciously, as if he had already known the answer.

"I am Aldric Chaudry. And you are?"

"I have a job for you."

"I apologize, good sir, I already have a job, one you've just tipped me very well for."

"Deservedly so, and yet, I do not care. How much coin do you make in a month's worth of performances?"

"Well now, don't you know that's rude to ask on a first date?"

"Even after all this time, I still do not understand your culture's nuances. In Ashar, every man's station determines his wage. Everyone of the same rank earns the same. There is no mystery in this. Regardless, I'm willing to pay you a monthly stipend for you to find a person for me."

"Ok, sweetheart, you have my attention. Tell me more about this monthly stipend?"

He obviously did not like the sweetheart. He provided me with a dramatic huff and stern look and replied, "You will get 100 silver every month for the next 1 year. You will be looking for a man who has been traveling for many years. He has lived many lives and knows more about life and the world than any other human ever to exist. He tends to be cranky and solitary; he will not like that I am giving you this job. Which is why you will not say anything about this meeting to anyone. Ever"

And of course, my lips have never breathed a word of that meeting to another living soul.
