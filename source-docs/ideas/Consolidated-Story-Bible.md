# **1. TIMELINE OF EVENTS IN STORY**

### **Year 0 (Beginning): Sero is a young scribe in Renmark, a coastal port city on western Caldor**

\- Works at a scribe shop

\- Meets and falls in love with Giana, who trains him

\- Smart but unmotivated; cynical about rulers

\- Wants freedom without ties

  

### **Year 0-1: The Asharan Empire conquers Renmark**

\- Empire from Keth (across the ocean) believes they are freeing the population

\- Militarily superior but prefers diplomacy over bloodshed

\- Executes twelve local leaders and demands fealty

\- Sero is captured while working late

  

### **Year 1-3: Sero becomes useful to the Empire**

\- Helps empire understand local records over 18 months

\- Meets Vashten, a translator and right hand to the Empress—becomes closest friend

\- Appointed to official position: record keeper, historian, information collector

\- Undergoes the Immortality Ritual (blood ceremony)

\- Begins traveling for the empire for 2-3 years

  

### **Year 3-5: The Plague and Loss of Giana**

\- Notices he doesn't age like others, doesn't get sick

\- Returns home to find Giana has aged; realizes immortality but no one had told him

\- The plague kills 25% of the population

\- Giana falls sick; Sero stays with her despite orders to flee

\- Giana dies on their porch. Her last words ask him to live a full life

\- Sero walks for two days, grieving

\- Learns from Vashten that he can only be killed by violence

  

### **Year \~50: Bard Arrives**

\- Junior scribe named Bard joins Sero

\- Had heard Sero described as a legend for 18 months before meeting

\- Shocked by Sero's cynicism

\- Decides to document Sero's life (like Geralt and Jaskier dynamic)

  

### **Year \~100-150: The Bard Incident**

\- Bard falls sick; Sero begs Vashten for help

\- Vashten arranges a partial ritual—Bard gains extended life but not immortality

\- Around year 150: Bard judges someone as untrustworthy

\- Sero doesn't believe him; person was planning to kill Sero

\- Bard exposes truth in a Socratic trial, knowing trial is rigged

\- Bard is executed but his monologue spreads across the world for centuries

\- Sero learns too late to trust Bard

\- Vows to see people as individuals capable of change

\- Preserves all Bard's writings

  

### **Year \~150-350: The Long Companionless Years**

\- Sero refuses another companion for nearly 200 years despite Vashten's urging

  

### **Year \~350-400: Vashten's Final Years**

\- Vashten is dying of old age; has served five Empresses

\- Final act: appoints young girl scribe named Nyneave to find Sero and bring him home

\- Vashten tells Sero about Nyneave on his deathbed

\- Vashten was the last person who knew Sero's original name

  

### **Year \~400-450: Nyneave's Era**

\- Nyneave travels with Sero for about 50 years

\- Becomes first person assigned to protect the archives

\- Archives have proximity effect: extend life to those who stay within them

\- When someone leaves, they age rapidly based on how old they are

\- Nyneave retires to care for an archive

  

### **Year \~450-700: Nyneave's Archive Life**

\- Nyneave lives in the archive for 250 years

\- Unknown to others: archives extend life to residents

  

### **Year \~700: Nyneave's Death**

\- Leaves the archive around year 700

\- Dies within months due to rapid aging from extended time in archive

  

### **Year \~700-1100: The Long Collapse**

\- The Asharan Empire crumbles completely around year 1100

\- Wars and chaos spread across Caldor

\- Sero must protect archives from destruction and relocate knowledge

\- For 500 years: no single power rules; dozens of kingdoms in constant flux (think 1500-1600 feudal Japan)

\- Sero becomes a wanderer, less attached to any state

\- Develops reputation as the neutral chronicler who serves no king

\- Daily life: similar to Crusades era—starvation, disease, vandals, armies

  

### **Year \~1100-1600: The Fractured Kingdoms Era**

\- New stable civilizations emerge around year 1600

\- Caldor fractures into 5-6 competing kingdoms

\- Centuries of warfare

\- Asharan language persists as trade lingua franca like Latin

  

### **Year \~1600: Sero Becomes Ancient Legend**

\- Sero is ancient now, a legend, half-believed by most

\- Begins seriously thinking about succession

  

### **Year \~2000 (Present Day): The Current Search**

\- Main novel takes place around year 2000 or later

\- Sero travels with two young men

\- At taverns, the boys ask for real stories

\- Sero tells other people's stories, never his origin

\- Boy A loves the strategy and cleverness in the tales

\- Boy B fixates on the human cost

\- Between taverns, Boy A dies

\- Only Sero and Boy B enter the next tavern

\- A patron recognizes Sero by an old title

\- Boy B realizes Sero has lived far longer than normal

\- Sero finally tells his real origin story

\- Offers Boy B the apprenticeship (succession)

\- Boy B refuses: "I will carry your stories as far as an ordinary life allows, and I will tell people the truth where I can. But I cannot be what you want me to be."

# **2. WORLDBUILDING**

## **2.1 Locations & Places**

MAREN

The world/planet where the entire story takes place. Medieval technology and society that evolves dramatically over 2,000 years.

  

CALDOR (Main Continent)

The primary continent where most stories take place. Pangea-style continental layout with three major continents plus 1-2 smaller ones a few days to weeks apart by ship.

\- Weather: Temperate; has a calmer sea on western side

\- Geography: Needs rough sketch of internal regions (mountains, plains, rivers, forests) to anchor story locations

\- Politics: Gradually fragments from unified empire rule into competing kingdoms

  

RENMARKA coastal port city on western Caldor

\- Mediterranean feel

\- Faces the calmer sea toward Keth

\- Where Sero was born and lived

\- Conquered by the Asharan Empire at the story's beginning

\- Home to the scribe shop where Sero met Giana

  

CAELDRATH

A massive walled coastal kingdom on the far side of Caldor

\- Remained UNCONQUERED by the Asharan Empire (only kingdom to resist successfully)

\- Ruled by one family for centuries

\- Genuinely good governance (contrasts with Asharan approach)

\- Hundreds of miles of walls closing off a massive area against the ocean

  

KETH

The homeland continent of the Asharan Empire

\- Hotter, drier climate

\- Closer to the equator than Caldor

\- Where the Empress and imperial capital reside

\- More protected from the chaos of the Long Collapse

  

MOSSEND

Third continent, virtually uninhabited

\- Vast forests

\- Tropical regions

\- Extraordinary wildlife

\- Civilization doesn't reach it until hundreds of years after Sero first visits

  

THE STAR-FARERS ARCHIVE

One special archive—origin unknown (no one knows where the ritual originally came from)

\- Home to advanced knowledge: chemistry, medicine, materials science

\- Only one Star-Farer archive exists

\- All other archives are from human history and various civilizations/societies

\- Note: The archive proximity effect ONLY applies to the one special Empire archive where Nyneave lived, NOT to Star-Farer or other archives

  

MULTIPLE EMPIRE ARCHIVES

Archives scattered across Caldor and Keth to preserve knowledge

\- Sero must protect them from destruction during the Long Collapse

\- Some get ransacked and must be investigated

\- Assigned archivists live within them and receive extended life benefits

\- When archivists leave, they age rapidly based on how long they stayed

  

TAVERNS (Throughout the World and Time)

\- Key locations where Sero tells stories to young men

\- Where the main novel's climax appears to occur

\- Where Boy B recognizes that Sero is far older than he seems

## **2.2 People & Characters**

SERO (THE TRAVELER)

ProtagonistReal Name: Unknown (only Vashten knew it)

Age at Immortality: Early-to-mid 20s (appears to be early 30s forever)

  

Physical Appearance: Average-looking man, not striking or ugly, never changes in appearance

Background:

\- Young scribe in Renmark before the conquest

\- Smart but unmotivated

\- Cynical about rulers, wanted freedom without ties

\- Loved deeply: married to Giana, wanted to live simply

\- Captured during Asharan conquest but treated well

  

Personality & Inspirations:

\- Inspired by: Spike Spiegel (Cowboy Bebop), Vash the Stampede (Trigun), Mat Cauthon (Wheel of Time), Miyamoto Musashi

\- Capable and wise but doesn't take himself too seriously

\- Witty, blunt, rude in ways most find off-putting

\- Accumulated immense knowledge over centuries

\- Still makes mistakes—when he's wrong, people die

\- Draws and writes constantly (journal always with him)

\- Learned to draw at roughly age 75; started as terrible but improved over centuries

  

Core Flaw:

\- Genuinely doesn't understand people

\- His knowledge alienates him

\- Rude and direct in ways that destroy relationships

\- Judges people for being ordinary, then judges himself for judging them

  

Character Arc:

\- Early (\~0-150 years): Strict Musashi-style code and discipline

\- Middle (\~150-1000 years): Deepening cynicism and apathy

\- Late (\~1000-2000 years): Tired, searching, unable to stop workingInternal 

  

Conflict:

\- Sees clearly and can't stop seeing

\- Watches humanity repeat patterns across millennia

\- Questions whether preserving failures serves purpose if they never learn

\- Contemplates ending it but realizes he can't

\- All the knowledge dies with him if he does

  

Abilities:

\- "Awake" consciousness—heightened awareness, uses more of his mind than others

\- Advanced knowledge: chemistry, medicine, materials science (from archives)

\- Convinces through logic and Socratic questioning

\- Never gets sick or ages

\- Heals fast from wounds

\- Can ONLY be killed by violence (unnatural death)

  

Operational Security:

\- Keeps very few relationships, only with people he trusts completely

\- Disappears from regions for decades, returns with new names

\- Cover identities help him hide the fact he never ages

\- Carries: journal, writing materials, basic road gear, drawing materials

\- Keeps small locked case with irreplaceable items (NOT the photograph—that stays in archive)

\- Uses cipher or dead language for sensitive writings

  

Greatest Fear: 

Dies before finding a successor; all knowledge is lost

Greatest Regret: Not trusting Bard

## **2.3 Events & History**

THE ASHARAN CONQUEST & RISE

Philosophy:- The Asharan Empire believes they are FREEING conquered people from terrible rule, not conquering them

\- The conquered are seen as "lost subjects who didn't know they belonged"

\- This is a benevolent worldview that masks imperialism

Method of Conquest:

\- Militarily superior to Caldor

\- Prefer diplomacy over bloodshed

\- Minimal violence in most conquests

\- Standard procedure: Execute leadership, demand fealty, improve infrastructure

  

Renmark's Conquest (Year 0):- Empire executes twelve local leaders

\- Demands fealty from everyone else

\- Sero is captured while working late (treated unharmed)

\- Represents the "gentle" side of Asharan conquest

  

Asharan Achievements & Rule:

\- More advanced than Caldor in almost every respect

\- Infrastructure improvements: canal systems, aqueducts

\- Better law, medicine, trade, education systems

\- Formal schools established

\- Poverty and illness genuinely improved under their rule

\- Population sees tangible benefits despite loss of independence

\- Trade language becomes Asharan (like Latin in real world)

  

Peak Reach: Controlled 50-70% of Caldor at height

\- Only Caeldrath remained unconquered

\- Presence on Keth (their homeland) continues longer than Caldor presence

  

THE PLAGUE WAVE

Timing: Several years after Renmark's conquest (around year 3-5)

Cause: Disease brought by empire's second wave of settlers

Death Toll: Kills 25% of population

Impact on Individuals:

\- Giana falls sick and dies

\- Marks turning point for Sero—forced to face his immortality

\- Sero breaks quarantine to stay with Giana (personal cost of his choice)

  

THE BARD'S TRIAL & EXECUTION

Timing: Around year 150The Incident:

\- Bard judges someone as untrustworthy

\- Sero dismisses Bard's judgment

\- The person Bard warned about was actually planning to kill Sero

\- Bard discovers and exposes the plot

  

The Trial:

\- Authorities arrest and try Bard for the exposure (rigged trial)

\- Bard knows he'll be executed

\- Uses the trial as a platform for truth-telling

\- Delivers a monologue about truth, trust, and the nature of the world

\- His words are Socratic—using questions to reveal deeper truths

  

The Execution & Legacy:

\- Bard is executed

\- His monologue spreads across the world and persists for centuries

\- Sero witnesses his death

\- Sero's great regret: not trusting Bard when it mattered

\- Vows to see people as individuals capable of change

\- Preserves everything Bard wrote

\- Adds his own account, calling Bard a better man than himself

\- Bard becomes immortal through his words, ironically more influential dead than alive

  

THE EMPIRE'S DECLINE & COLLAPSE

The Tyrant Era (Year \~800-820, approximate):

\- Fourth Empress becomes a tyrant over course of 60-70 years

\- Institutes slavery throughout empire

\- Treats all subjects brutally

\- Exploitation reaches unsustainable levels

  

The Collapse Cascade:

\- Population revolts against the tyrant

\- Revolt takes 60-70 years as resistance builds

\- Tyrant is eventually killed

\- His death triggers complete collapse of Asharan rule on Caldor (around year 1100)

  

Result: Caldor fractures into 5-6 competing kingdoms in constant conflict

  

THE LONG COLLAPSE (Year \~700-1100)

Duration: 400 years of chaos and fragmentation

Conditions During This Era:

\- No single power rules Caldor

\- Dozens of kingdoms exist in constant flux

\- Daily life similar to Crusades period

\- Starvation, disease, vandals

\- Constant warfare between competing powers

\- Terrible conditions for ordinary people

\- Archives are targeted for destruction (knowledge as power)

  

Sero's Role:

\- Must protect archives from destruction

\- Must relocate knowledge to safer locations

\- Becomes increasingly detached from any single state

\- Develops reputation as neutral chronicler who serves no king

\- Travels constantly, witnessing the collapse

  

Asharan Persistence:

\- Empire on Keth survives but contracts slowly

\- Asharan language persists as trade lingua franca across the fractured kingdoms

\- Culture outlasts politics (like Latin in medieval Europe)

  

THE FRACTURED ERA (Year \~1100-1600)

Conditions:

\- 5-6 competing kingdoms emerge on Caldor after chaos settles somewhat

\- Centuries of warfare between them

\- Gradually more stable but still fragmented

\- New civilizations develop from the ashes

  

Around Year 1600:

\- Stable civilizations begin to truly re-establish themselves

\- End of the absolute chaos phase- Beginning of new cultural developments

\- Sero becomes ancient legend, half-believed

\- Begins thinking seriously about succession

  

THE STAR-FARER MYSTERY

The Ritual's Origin:

\- No one knows where the immortality ritual originally came from

\- Empire has "always" done it—no recorded beginning

\- Could be: Star-Farer technology, empire's own discovery, something older

\- Never explained to Sero (kept secret by Empress's order)

  

The Star-Farer Archive:

\- One special archive exists with advanced knowledge

\- Contains: chemistry, medicine, materials science, other advanced concepts

\- Origin and purpose unclear (part of the mystery)

\- Distinct from all human history archives

## **2.4 Beliefs, Cultures, Societies & Religions**

ASHARAN EMPIRE IDEOLOGY

Core Belief System:

\- Paternalistic imperialism disguised as liberation

\- View of themselves: Enlightened, civilizing force

\- View of conquered: Subjects who don't understand they're lost without the empire

\- Central claim: They're freeing people, not subjugating them

  

Cultural Superiority Complex:

\- Genuinely more advanced in technology, infrastructure, law, medicine

\- This advancement lends credibility to their ideology

\- Subjects do experience material improvement

\- Creates moral confusion: Is this liberation or imperialism? Both?

  

Governance Ideology:

\- Empress is supreme ruler but advised by council

\- Council includes priests/shamans

\- Long-term rule (250 years per empress) allows for consistent policy

\- Succession by selection (not blood)—can corrupt or improve based on council choices

  

The Immortality Ritual:

\- Religious/magical ceremony involving blood-wine

\- Performed by priests under empress's command

\- Kept secret from recipients

\- Seen as a burden, not a gift (by priests)

\- Grant of immortality is a form of control and assignment

  

CALDOR (LOCAL) CULTURES BEFORE CONQUEST

Renmark Culture:

\- Coastal, maritime focus

\- Mediterranean feel and values

\- Scribal tradition (writing and record-keeping important)

\- Simple life valued (Sero's preference before conquest)

\- Some form of local governance (twelve leaders executed by Asharan)

\- Poorer, less developed than Asharan Empire but established

  

Other Caldor Kingdoms:

\- Varying levels of development

\- Some genuinely terrible governance (justifying Asharan "liberation" narrative)

\- Some better, more stable

\- Cultural diversity across the continent

  

CAELDRATH CULTURE (The Resisting Kingdom)

Governance:

\- Ruled by one family for generations

\- Genuinely good governance (documented in comparison to Asharan approach)

\- Values independence and autonomy

\- Willing to wage war to maintain freedom

\- Successfully resists Asharan military superiority through walls and strategy

  

Values:

\- Self-determination

\- Resistance to outside control

\- Community-based rather than empire-based

\- Preservation of local culture and autonomy

  

Military:

\- Defensive warfare

\- Massive walls as primary defense

\- Strategic positioning on coast

\- Successfully maintains sovereignty

  

KETH CULTURE (Asharan Homeland)
