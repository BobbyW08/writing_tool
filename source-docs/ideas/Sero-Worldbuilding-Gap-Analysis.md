**PRIORITY DECISIONS — WHERE TO START**

*The following decisions will unlock the most other decisions. Tackle these first.*

**1. Geography —** Rough continental layout — how many, where, what separates them. Everything else depends on this.

**2. Sero's homeland —** What was his city like? Mediterranean port? River delta civilization? This grounds the origin story.

**3. The empire's origin —** Where do they come from and what makes them different? This is the inciting event of the whole saga.

**4. The ritual mechanics —** Exactly how does it work and can it be transferred? This drives the entire successor plotline.

**5. Post-empire world —** What does the world look like after the collapse? Fragmented kingdoms or one dominant successor?

**6. Real-world cultural analogues —** Which ancient civilizations are you borrowing from for each region? Pick a handful — this will make worldbuilding much faster.

**7. Sero's appearance and cover identities —** How does he explain not aging across generations? This is a practical problem he's had to solve 2,000 times.

**8. The trade language —** Is there a lingua franca? This affects every scene where Sero talks to strangers.
